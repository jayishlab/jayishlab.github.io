Questions, Answered

Do my sequences get uploaded to a server?

No. Every calculation runs inside your browser. Your data never leaves your computer.

Is Jayish Lab free to use?

Yes — free forever, and open source under the MIT license.

Can I cite Jayish Lab tools in my thesis?

Absolutely. Each tool page lists the formulas used, so you can cite them with confidence.
