Primer Checker

Evaluate PCR primers instantly: length, GC%, Tm, GC-clamp and self-dimer warnings. All calculations run in your browser — no sequence is ever uploaded.

Educational tool. Tm uses the Wallace rule (<14 nt) or the salt-adjusted formula Tm = 64.9 + 41 x (G+C - 16.4)/N. For rigorous design, validate with NCBI Primer-BLAST or Primer3.
