Restriction Cutter

Paste a DNA sequence and the tool scans every enzyme in its database, ranks them by cut sites, and flags the best single cutters. All in your browser.


Limitations: this tool recognises palindromic recognition sites only; cut positions are assumed at the start of the site (overhang offsets are not modelled); star activity, methylation and buffer conditions are not considered. For cloning decisions, verify results with NEBcutter or supplier documentation. Educational simulation only.
