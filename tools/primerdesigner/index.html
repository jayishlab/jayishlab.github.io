Primer Designer

Upload a FASTA template and get ranked primer pairs — designed with Primer3-style heuristic filtering, fully in your browser. No uploads, no accounts.

Method: candidates (18-22 nt) filtered by Tm 57-63 C (salt-adjusted formula), GC 40-60%, 3-prime GC-clamp, no homopolymer runs, no self-complementarity; pairs require product 100-1000 bp and Tm difference ≤2 C; ranked by penalty score (concept after Primer3, Untergasser et al. 2012). Educational tool — confirm specificity with NCBI Primer-BLAST before ordering primers.
